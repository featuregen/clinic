1. Getting Started

Welcome to FeatureGen Care! To log into your clinic's portal:

  1. Navigate to your clinic's specific web address (e.g., yourclinic.featuregen.com).
  2. Enter your assigned Username and Password.
  3. Upon logging in, you will be directed to your role-specific dashboard.
Security Tip: Your session is highly secure. If you are inactive for an extended period, the system will safely log you out to protect patient data.

2. The Patient Journey (End-to-End Workflow)

Here is how a standard patient visit flows through the software:

1. Registration & Booking (Receptionist)

The patient arrives. The receptionist clicks "Add Patient", enters basic details, and clicks "Book Appointment" to assign them to a Doctor for a specific time slot.

2. Consultation (Doctor)

The Doctor sees the appointment on their Dashboard. They open the patient's file, review past history, and click "Create Prescription" or use the Dental/Vaccination Chart.

3. Checkout & Billing (Accountant/Receptionist)

The patient returns to the front desk. The receptionist clicks "Create Invoice", adds the consultation fee and any procedures, collects the payment, and prints the final receipt.

3. Guide for Receptionists

1 Managing Patients

  • Adding a Patient: Go to the sidebar > Patients > Add Patient. Fill in the required fields (Name, Phone, Age, Gender) and Save.
  • Searching: Use the universal search bar at the top of the dashboard to quickly find patients by name or phone number.

2 Booking Appointments

  • Go to Appointments > Book Appointment.
  • Select the Patient (or add a new one directly from the booking screen).
  • Choose the Doctor, Date, and an available Time Slot.
  • Click Book. The appointment will instantly appear on the Doctor's dashboard.

3 Queue Management

From the Appointments List, you can change the status of an appointment to keep track of the waiting room:

  • Scheduled: Patient has booked.
  • Completed: Patient has seen the doctor.
  • Cancelled: Patient did not show up.

4. Guide for Doctors

1 Dashboard & Patient Queue

When you log in, your dashboard shows your appointments for the day. Click on an appointment to view the patient's complete medical profile, past visits, and uploaded documents.

2 Writing Prescriptions (E-Rx)

  • From the patient's profile, click Create Prescription.
  • Add medicines by typing the name, selecting dosage (e.g., 1-0-1), and duration.
  • Add general advice, chief complaints, and diagnosis.
  • Click Save & Print to generate a professional PDF prescription.
Pro Tip: Go to Templates on the sidebar to create "Prescription Templates" for common illnesses (like Viral Fever). You can then apply these templates to a patient with just one click to save time!

3 Specialized Modules

  • Dental Charting: Open a patient's profile and click the "Dental" tab. Click on a specific tooth on the visual chart to log conditions (Decay, Missing) or treatments (RCT, Extraction).
  • Vaccinations: Open the "Vaccination" tab to see the child's immunization schedule, mark doses as given, and log batch numbers.

5. Guide for Clinic Administrators

1 Clinic Settings

Go to Administration > Clinic Settings to update your clinic's Name, Address, Contact Info, and Logo. This information automatically appears on all printed prescriptions and invoices.

2 Managing Master Data

Before your clinic runs smoothly, you should populate dropdown menus:

  • Go to Administration > Master Data.
  • Add your clinic's Departments (e.g., General Medicine, Pediatrics).
  • Add Specialties (e.g., Cardiologist, Dentist).

3 Staff & Doctor Management

  • Add Doctors: Go to Management > Doctors > Add Doctor. Set up their login credentials, specialty, consultation fees, and appointment slot durations.
  • Add Staff: Go to Management > Staff > Add Staff. Assign roles like Receptionist, Accountant, or Nurse.
Security Check: The System Administrator account cannot be edited or deleted by normal users, ensuring you never get locked out of your system.
