1. Executive Summary

FeatureGen Care is a state-of-the-art, multi-tenant Clinic Management System designed to digitize, streamline, and secure the daily operations of modern medical facilities. From a single unified platform, healthcare providers can manage patients, appointments, digital prescriptions, complex billing, and multi-disciplinary specialty charts (including dental and vaccination modules).

Architecture Note: The platform operates on a robust Multi-Tenant (SaaS) architecture. Every registered clinic gets its own isolated database linked via a custom subdomain (e.g., apollo.featuregen.com), guaranteeing maximum data privacy, security, and independent scalability.

2. Core Modules & Capabilities

2.1 Advanced Patient Management

A centralized hub for all patient demographics and medical histories.

  • Digital Patient Records: Capture comprehensive demographics, contact details, and initial vital signs.
  • Medical History Timeline: View a patient's complete history including past visits, prescriptions, and billing ledgers in one click.
  • Smart Search: Global AJAX-powered search to instantly locate patients by name, ID, or phone number.

Eliminate waiting room bottlenecks with a digital reception desk.

  • Doctor Scheduling: Book appointments based on doctor availability, assigned departments, and customized slot durations.

2.3 Digital Prescriptions (E-Rx)

Fast, secure, and professional prescription generation.

  • Smart Templates: Doctors can create and save prescription templates for common ailments to prescribe in one click.

4. Administrative & System Controls

4.1 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Strict security boundaries ensure staff only see what they need to see.

  • Super Admin: Oversees the entire SaaS network, provisions new clinics, and manages overarching system health. (Highly restricted)
  • Clinic Admin: Manages clinic settings, master data, and staff permissions.
  • Doctors: Access clinical data, write prescriptions, and view their schedule.
  • Receptionists: Handle bookings, basic patient registration, and queue management.
  • Accountants: Manage billing, invoices, and financial reports.

4.2 Multi-Tenant Provisioning

  • Automated Onboarding: Provisioning script seamlessly creates isolated databases and auto-runs schema configurations for newly onboarded clinics.
  • Session Isolation: Secure cookie management prevents cross-tenant session hijacking or data bleeding.

5. Technical Specifications

  • Backend Ecosystem: Modern PHP 8+ with PDO for secure database abstraction.
  • Database: MySQL 8+ distributed architecture (Master Routing DB + Isolated Tenant DBs).
